Timekeeper Support

Timekeeper should stay easy to use, especially when an alarm is important.

Alarm did not sound?

  1. Open Timekeeper → Alarms. For a verified iOS 26 system alarm, look for Registered in iOS; on iOS 17–25, look for Notification queued. A saved enabled switch alone does not prove iOS scheduled an alert or that an alert will sound.
  2. In Timekeeper Settings, check the alarm permission status. On iOS 26 or later, allow system alarms; on iOS 17–25, verify notification permissions in iPhone Settings and remember that Focus and Silent Mode can affect notifications.
  3. Check your device's sound output, battery, and iOS updates. Never rely on a single device as the only alert for safety-critical events.
  4. If you see Schedule not verified, Notification not verified, or Status unavailable, use the nearby Review/Retry status action. Review the saved alarm and re-save only when needed; Retry status reads iOS status without scheduling an alarm.

Plus purchase missing?

  1. Ensure you're signed into the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open Timekeeper → Settings → Restore Purchases.
  3. If the purchase remains unavailable, check your Apple subscription settings and try again.

Report a problem

Please include Timekeeper's app version, your iOS version, the device model, and steps to reproduce. Do not include private alarm labels, passwords, payment information, or personal data.

Open a support ticket (requires a GitHub account)

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Apple's App Store handles payments and refunds.
