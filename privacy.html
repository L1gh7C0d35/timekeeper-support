Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Timekeeper is a local-first iOS clock and alarm application. You do not need an account to use the app. We do not operate a Timekeeper account server or advertising network.

Information stored on your device

Saved alarms and wake preferences, timers, selected world cities, stopwatch sessions, purchase-entitlement state, and wake activity records are maintained on your device. Timekeeper does not transmit these records to our servers. You can remove entries inside the app or remove all app data by deleting the app.

Permissions and sensors

Alarm permission is used to schedule Apple system alarms and timers. Camera access is requested only for a QR or barcode-based wake challenge; scanning is performed on the device. Motion access is used only for optional movement and step wake challenges. You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Timekeeper does not upload camera or motion recordings.

Calendar suggestions

If you explicitly choose to connect your calendar, Timekeeper requests read access to upcoming events solely to suggest an alarm time using your selected preparation and travel buffers. Event titles and times are processed on your device and are not sent to Timekeeper servers. Calendar access is optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings. No suggested alarm is scheduled without your review and Save action.

In-app purchases

Optional premium purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store billing. Apple handles payment processing and purchase history. Timekeeper reads verified transaction entitlements to unlock features; we do not receive or store payment card numbers. Apple's handling of Apple Account and transaction information is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Advertising and tracking

The current iOS app contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK and does not use cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Support requests

If you choose to contact support using GitHub Issues, information you submit may be public and is handled under GitHub's policies. Do not include sensitive personal information, precise addresses, payment data, or account credentials.

Policy changes

Optional future integrations may require additional permissions or third-party services. This policy will be updated before those features are released. The current version will remain available here.

Contact

Use the Timekeeper support page for privacy questions or issue reports.
