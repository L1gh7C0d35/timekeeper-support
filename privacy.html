Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

Timekeeper is a local-first iOS clock and alarm application. You do not need an account to use the app. We do not operate a Timekeeper account server or advertising network.

Information stored on your device

Saved alarms and wake preferences, timers, selected world cities, stopwatch sessions, personal scenes, recordings, purchase-entitlement state, and wake activity records are maintained in app-private storage on your device. Timekeeper does not transmit these records to its own servers. Depending on your device settings, iOS or computer backups may include some saved app data, such as imported audio or personal wake photos. Timekeeper requests backup exclusion for temporary recordings and checkpoint images, but cannot remove earlier device backups or independently shared copies. You can remove saved entries inside the app or remove local app data by deleting the app. Exported or deliberately shared files are managed by the destination you choose and may remain there after deleting Timekeeper.

Optional permissions and on-device features

Alarms and notifications: Used to schedule system alarms, countdowns and alerts you create. On supported versions of iOS, Timekeeper uses Apple's AlarmKit; on older systems, some alert experiences use local notifications.

Camera and photos: Used only when you choose QR/barcode wake missions, photo checkpoints, or on-device object-recognition challenges. The camera input is processed on your device. If you select your own wake photo or scene, Timekeeper accesses the image you select using Apple's photo picker. We do not upload camera frames, selected wake photos or challenge images to Timekeeper servers.

Motion and NFC: Used only for movement/step missions or NFC checkpoint challenges that you deliberately enable. NFC tag identifiers and challenge preferences may be stored on your device for later alarms; Timekeeper does not receive them on a server.

Microphone and audio: Microphone permission is used only when you start an optional night audio recording. Recordings and any on-device analysis stay on your iPhone until you delete or explicitly export them. Selected alarm sounds or ambient audio may play in the background according to iOS permissions.

Location: Optional foreground location access helps select a current city for World Clock. Timekeeper does not run background location tracking or upload precise coordinates to Timekeeper servers.

Apple Health: If you connect sleep insights, Timekeeper requests read access to supported sleep records through HealthKit. Timekeeper does not write or upload those records. You can disconnect access in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

All of these optional permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings or the Apple Health app. If you disable a permission, its corresponding feature may stop working until you re-enable it.

Calendar suggestions and meeting creation

Calendar access is optional and initiated by you. If you choose calendar wake suggestions, Timekeeper requests full read access to upcoming events to propose an alarm time using your preparation and travel buffers. Event titles and times are processed on your device, not sent to a Timekeeper server, and no suggested alarm is scheduled without your review and Save action. Separately, if you confirm Add 30-minute event in the World Clock Time-Zone Planner, Timekeeper requests write-only Calendar permission to add one meeting at the chosen time to your default calendar, with the selected city names in its notes. That action does not read your existing calendar events. You can revoke Calendar permissions in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases

Optional premium purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store billing. Apple handles payment processing and purchase history. Timekeeper reads verified transaction entitlements to unlock features; we do not receive or store payment card numbers. Apple's handling of Apple Account and transaction information is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Advertising and tracking

The current iOS app contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK and does not use cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Support requests

If you choose to contact support using GitHub Issues, information you submit may be public and is handled under GitHub's policies. Do not include sensitive personal information, precise addresses, payment data, or account credentials.

Policy changes

Optional future integrations may require additional permissions or third-party services. This policy will be updated before those features are released. The current version will remain available here.
