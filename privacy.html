Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026

Timekeeper is a local-first iOS clock and alarm application. You do not need an account to use the app. We do not operate a Timekeeper account server or advertising network.

Information stored on your device

Saved alarms and wake preferences, timers, selected world cities, stopwatch sessions, recordings, purchase-entitlement state, and wake activity records are maintained on your device. Timekeeper does not transmit these records to its own servers. You can remove saved entries inside the app or remove local app data by deleting the app. Exported or deliberately shared files are managed by the destination you choose and may remain there after deleting Timekeeper.

Optional permissions and on-device features

Alarms and notifications: Used to schedule system alarms, countdowns and alerts you create. Timekeeper requires iOS 26 or later and uses Apple's AlarmKit for supported system alarm and timer alerts.

Camera and photos: Used only when you choose QR/barcode wake missions or photo checkpoints. Camera input and reference images are processed on your device. If you choose a reference photo, Timekeeper handles the photo locally. We do not upload camera frames or challenge images to Timekeeper servers.

Motion and NFC: Used only for movement/step missions or NFC checkpoint challenges that you deliberately enable. NFC tag identifiers and challenge preferences may be stored on your device for later alarms; Timekeeper does not receive them on a server.

Microphone and audio: Microphone permission is used only when you start an optional night audio recording. Recordings and any on-device analysis stay on your iPhone until you delete or explicitly export them. Selected alarm sounds or ambient audio may play in the background according to iOS permissions.

World Clock: Timekeeper starts with your device's configured time zone and lets you choose additional cities. Its core World Clock does not request GPS location or run background location tracking.

Apple Health: If you connect sleep insights, Timekeeper requests read access to supported sleep records through HealthKit. Timekeeper does not write or upload those records. You can disconnect access in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

The optional microphone, camera, motion, NFC, Calendar, Health and alarm permissions that apply to your enabled features can be managed in iOS Settings or the Apple Health app. If you disable a permission, its corresponding feature may stop working until you re-enable it.

Calendar suggestions

If you explicitly choose to connect your calendar, Timekeeper requests read access to upcoming events solely to suggest an alarm time using your selected preparation and travel buffers. Event titles and times are processed on your device and are not sent to Timekeeper servers. Calendar access is optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings. No suggested alarm is scheduled without your review and Save action.

In-app purchases

Optional premium purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store billing. Apple handles payment processing and purchase history. Timekeeper reads verified transaction entitlements to unlock features; we do not receive or store payment card numbers. Apple's handling of Apple Account and transaction information is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Advertising and tracking

The current iOS app contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK and does not use cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Support requests

If you choose to contact support using GitHub Issues, information you submit may be public and is handled under GitHub's policies. Do not include sensitive personal information, precise addresses, payment data, or account credentials.

Policy changes

Optional future integrations may require additional permissions or third-party services. This policy will be updated before those features are released. The current version will remain available here.
